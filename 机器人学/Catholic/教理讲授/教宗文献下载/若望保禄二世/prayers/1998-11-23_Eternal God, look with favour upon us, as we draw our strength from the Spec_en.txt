Eternal God,

look with favour upon us,

as we draw our strength

from the Special Assembly

of the Synod of Bishops for Oceania

to welcome the new millennium

in Jesus Christ.

He is our Word become flesh

who invites us to walk his way, to tell

his truth and to live his life.

We, the peoples of Oceania,

diverse in our ways,

and scattered over a large area,

pledge to proclaim his love

to the world, to search for those

gone astray and to lead them to you.

Mary, Queen of Peace,

intercede for us your children,

as we make this prayer in Jesus' name.

Amen.
