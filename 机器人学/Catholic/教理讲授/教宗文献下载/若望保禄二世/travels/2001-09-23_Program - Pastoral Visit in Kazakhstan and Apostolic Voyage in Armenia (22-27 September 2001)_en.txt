PASTORAL VISIT

OF HIS HOLINESS JOHN PAUL II

IN KAZAKHSTAN

AND APOSTOLIC VOYAGE IN ARMENIA

ON THE OCCASION OF THE 1700 YEARS

OF CHRISTIANITY IN THE COUNTRY

22-27 SEPTEMBER 2001

PROGRAM

ITALY

Saturday, 22 September 2001

08.30 a.m.

DEPARTURE by airplane from "Leonardo da Vinci" International Airport of Fiumicino (Rome) for Astana.

KAZAKHSTAN

Astana

7.30 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the International Airport of Astana.

WELCOME CEREMONY at the International Airport of Astana. Speech by the Holy Father (1).

8.45 p.m.

VISIT TO THE MONUMENT FOR THE VICTIMS OF THE TOTALITARIAN REGIME of Astana.

Sunday, 23 September 2001

10.05 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Mother of the Homeland Square of Astana.

10.30 a.m.

MASS at the Mother of the Homeland Square of Astana. Homily by the Holy Father (2).

PRAYER OF THE ANGELUS DOMINI at the Mother of the Homeland Square of Astana. Words by the Holy Father (3).

2.00 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Apostolic Nunciature of Astana.

MEETING AND LUNCH WITH THE ORDINARIES OF CENTRAL ASIA AND WITH THE CARDINALS AND BISHOPS OF THE PAPAL ENTOURAGE at the Apostolic Nunciature of Astana. Speech by the Holy Father (4).

5.30 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Presidential Palace of Astana.

COURTESY VISIT TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC at the Presidential Palace of Astana.

6.30 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Eurasia University of Astana.

6.45 p.m.

YOUTH MEETING in the Aula Magna of the Eurasia University of Astana. Speech by the Holy Father (5).

Monday, 24 September 2001

09.45 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Cathedral of the Vergin of Perpetual Help of Astana.

10.00 a.m.

MASS WITH PRIESTS, RELIGIOUS AND SEMINARIANS at the Cathedral of the Vergin of Perpetual Help of Astana. Homily by the Holy Father (6).

1.00 p.m.

Private lunch with the Bishops guests and the Members of the Papal Entourage, in the Bishop’s House of Astana.

6.00 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Congress Hall of Astana.

MEETING WITH REPRESENTATIVES OF THE WORLD OF CULTURE, ARTS AND SCIENCE in the Auditorium of the Congress Hall of Astana. Speech by the Holy Father (7).

Tuesday, 25 September 2001

07.30 a.m.

Holy Mass in private in the Chapel of the Apostolic Nunciature of Astana.

09.45 a.m.

Farewell from the Apostolic Nunciature of Astana.

10.30 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the International Airport of Astana.

FAREWELL CEREMONY at the International Airport of Astana. Greetings by the Holy Father (8).

11.00 a.m.

DEPARTURE by airplane from the International Airport of Astana for Yerevan.

ARMENIA

Yerevan

1.00 p.m.

ARRIVAL at Zvartnotz International Airport of Yerevan.

WELCOME CEREMONY at Zvartnotz International Airport of Yerevan. Speech by the Holy Father (9).

Etchmiadzin

2.15 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Apostolic See of Etchmiadzin.

PRAYER VISIT at the Apostolic Cathedral of Etchmiadzin. Greetings by the Holy Father (10).

6.00 p.m.

OFFICIAL MEETING WITH THE SUPREME PATRIARCH AND CATHOLICOS OF ALL ARMENIANS HIS HOLINESS KAREKIN II in the Great Hall of the Apostolic Palace of Etchmiadzin.

Wednesday, 26 September 2001

07.30 a.m.

Holy Mass in private in the Chapel of the Apostolic Palace of Etchmiadzin.

Yerevan

10.30 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Presidential Palace of Yerevan.

COURTESY VISIT TO THE PRESIDENT OF THE REPUBLIC in the Golden Room of the Presidential Palace of Yerevan.

11.30 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Memorial of Tzitzernagaberd at Yerevan.

VISIT TO THE MEMORIAL OF TZITZERNAGABERD at Yerevan.

Etchmiadzin

12.45 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Apostolic See of Etchmiadzin

1.00 p.m.

OFFICIAL LUNCH WITH THE SUPREME PATRIARCH AND CATHOLICOS OF ALL ARMENIANS HIS HOLINESS KAREKIN II, WITH ARCHBISHOPS AND BISHOPS OF THE ARMENIAN APOSTOLIC CHURCH AND WITH THE CARDINALS AND BISHOPS OF THE PAPAL ENTOURAGE in the Apostolic Palace of Etchmiadzin.

Yerevan

6.30 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the new Apostolic Cathedral of St. Gregory the Illuminator of Yerevan.

ECUMENICAL CELEBRATION in the new Apostolic Cathedral of St. Gregory the Illuminator of Yerevan. Speech by the Holy Father (11).

Thursday, 27 September 2001

Etchmiadzin

09.15 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Sacristy near the external Grand Altar of Etchmiadzin.

09.30 a.m.

HOLY MASS in Latin rite, at the external Grand Altar of Etchmiadzin. Homily by the Holy Father (12).

Yerevan

12.30 a.m.

ARRIVAL at the Armenian Catholic Center of Kanaker at Yerevan.

OFFICIAL LUNCH in the Armenian Catholic Center of Kanaker at Yerevan.

Etchmiadzin

4.30 p.m.

FAREWELL FROM THE APOSTOLIC CATHEDRAL OF ETCHMIADZIN.

Khor Virab

5.30 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Monastery of Khor Virab.

VISIT AT THE MONASTERY OF KHOR VIRAB.

Yerevan

6.45 p.m.

ARRIVAL at Zvartnotz International Airport of Yerevan.

FAREWELL CEREMONY at Zvartnotz International Airport of Yerevan. Greetings by the Holy Father (13).

7.30 p.m.

DEPARTURE by airplane from Zvartnotz International Airport di Yerevan for Rome.

ITALY

Ciampino (Rome)

8.45 p.m.

ARRIVAL at the Airport of Ciampino (Rome).
